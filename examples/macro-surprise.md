# Example — inflation surprise and asset-price response

**Prompt:** "CPI is above consensus. Must USD appreciate and equities fall?"

**Method:** Verify jurisdiction, headline/core print, release time, surprise versus vintage consensus, real rates and policy path. Explain the inflationary discount-rate channel and opposing growth/risk channels. Compare real yields, rates futures, FX quote convention and the observed windows. A positive CPI surprise does **not** logically imply a fixed asset response. Do not treat a release known after a market close as available before it.
