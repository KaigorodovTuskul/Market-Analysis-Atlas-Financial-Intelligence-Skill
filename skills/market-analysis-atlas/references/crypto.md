# Crypto market structure

Blockchain transactions and wallet addresses are observable but address clustering does not prove beneficial ownership. On-chain liquidity, bridge inflows, exchange deposit estimates and realized capital measures depend on provider heuristics. Market maker wash trades can inflate volume.

For perpetuals: inspect mark/index price methodology, funding calculation, exchange insurance funds, liquidation engine, collateral currency, cross margin and basis. Leverage-induced cascades invalidate linear risk intuitions.

For DeFi: audit contract upgrade privileges, oracle failure modes, lending utilization, protocol solvency, stablecoin redemption terms and governance concentration. TVL may be double-counted through wrapped and rehypothecated positions. Never identify a wallet as a person without robust lawful evidence.
