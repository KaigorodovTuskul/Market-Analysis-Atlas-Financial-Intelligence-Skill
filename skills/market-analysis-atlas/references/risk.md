# Adversarial verification checklist

- Would the conclusion flip if you change time frame, benchmark, corporate-action adjustment, data vintage or pivot tolerance?
- Is the pattern detected at the time it forms, or only after future bars arrive?
- Are observations independent? Has the strategy been tuned on the alleged test sample?
- Does an apparent alpha survive the benchmark, fees, taxes, financing, liquidity, funding, leverage and extreme-tail events?
- Could omitted accounting disclosures, non-cash gains or one-time tax adjustments explain earnings quality?
- Are shock paths symmetric? FX forwards can eliminate spot risk for hedged cash flows yet create margin, basis and early-redemption risks.
- What is the negative control or competing explanation? What data would falsify the thesis?
- Is any number or quote a third-party copyrighted original? Do not reproduce substantial prose or image assets from external sites.
