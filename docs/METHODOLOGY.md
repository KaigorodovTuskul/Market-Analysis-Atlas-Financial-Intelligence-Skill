# Sources, methodology and rights

## Inputs examined during authorship

- [Investopedia Financial Terms Dictionary](https://www.investopedia.com/financial-term-dictionary-4769738): directory navigation and topical inspiration.
- Verified alphabetic pages: [A](https://www.investopedia.com/terms-beginning-with-a-4769351), [B](https://www.investopedia.com/terms-beginning-with-b-4769352), [C](https://www.investopedia.com/terms-beginning-with-c-4769353), [D](https://www.investopedia.com/terms-beginning-with-d-4769354), [Z](https://www.investopedia.com/terms-beginning-with-z-4769376).
- Specific article link verification: [Head and Shoulders](https://www.investopedia.com/terms/h/head-shoulders.asp), [Bollinger Bands](https://www.investopedia.com/terms/b/bollingerbands.asp), [CAPE Ratio](https://www.investopedia.com/terms/c/cape-ratio.asp).
- [Pareto Forge repository](https://github.com/KaigorodovTuskul/pareto-forge) for packaging convention (compact YAML-frontmatter skill, lazy references, installer, validation and documentation).
- [Agent Skills specification](https://agentskills.io/specification) for interoperability goal.

The 533 definitions were written independently in the `scripts/build_seed.py` authoring source; no Investopedia passages or images were exported. Source links are **attribution/navigation, not a full evidence audit of every definition**. Technical parameters, formulas and methods are generic educational conventions which may differ from product, accounting or exchange implementations.

## Charts

100 SVGs regenerated from deterministic points and OHLC sketches. Every image includes synthetic/educational marking. These images are **not replicas** of original Investopedia art. No pattern success rates are assumed. There is no model calibration or detector ground truth.

## Copyright and website access

Copyright concerns the creative expression of articles and their figures, not ownership of fundamental financial concepts themselves. This project does not rehost the site's long-form texts or graphics. Collecting titles/links may still be subject to website terms, automation rules, database rights in some jurisdictions or negotiated licenses. The optional sync script must be used only when lawful. A permitted robots.txt path alone does not convey content-reuse rights.

## Contributions

New entries require independently written definitions, no close paraphrasing of protected text, evidence checks where material, explicit formula units and licensing provenance. Do not scrape or dump copyrighted article descriptions/images into PRs. Reviewers should verify examples and changes in finance conventions.
