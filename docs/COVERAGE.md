## What isn't covered

The supplied Investopedia dictionary also contains thousands of entries on taxes, estate law, insurance, personal finance, careers, US-specific forms, biographies, etc. It contains more technical and fundamental subtopics than this curated set. Entries are English, many short; the corpus is **not a synonym-complete or academic textbook-level encyclopedia**. There are no copied original-site passages, historical charts or licensed article images. No claims that every indexed article includes a chart.

## Completing a licensed full-scale collection

1. Obtain written reuse/data rights for full articles/images **or** use an explicitly reusable alternative corpus.
2. Build a canonical title/URL and license index; measure index counts, duplicates and dead links.
3. Independently author/peer-review definitions, formulas, domain tags and ambiguity notes for each entry, storing article-provenance separately.
4. Add genuinely useful, reproducible examples for visual terms, labeling simulated vs observed data.
5. Publish a machine-verifiable coverage manifest with `expected`, `retrieved`, `reviewed`, `illustrated`, `missing`, `rejected` counts and snapshot date.

Do not silently promote `index_only` titles to `definition_complete` or imply a source article was ingested from its title.
